# Tennis

Static concept site for a fictional tennis club. No build step: edit `index.html`, `styles.css` and `script.js` directly.

## Rules

- Keep the site clearly labelled as a concept in the footer.
- No pricing, testimonials, winner galleries or enroll buttons. Programs are shown as cards only.
- Photos load from the Unsplash CDN under the Unsplash License. Don't use template or stock-kit assets.
- Check every change at 375px and 1280px wide before committing.
